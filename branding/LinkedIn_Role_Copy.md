**Why it is built this way for ATS**

- The first line states ownership and scope in plain words — most parsers weight the opening heavily.
- Product names are in CAPS on their own line, so a human skims them and a parser reads them as headings.
- Every skill appears as the **exact string a recruiter searches for** — "attack surface management", "zero trust", "DevSecOps", "LLMOps", "NIS2", "MITRE ATT&CK" — not as a paraphrase.
- Named tools are spelled as vendors spell them (`FastAPI`, `PostgreSQL`, `MITRE ATT&CK`, `NIST SP 800-207`). Parsers do literal matching; a typo is an unmatched keyword.
- No tables, no columns, no images, no header/footer text — the four things that break ATS extraction.
