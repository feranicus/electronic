Why triangulation, and not more alerts

Every one of these sensors fires constantly on its own. A monitoring programme dies the week its queue stops being readable.

1
Signal · noise

A wallet moved. An agent travelled. A file was opened. Each happens hundreds of times a day and means nothing alone. Alert on this and you will be muted by Friday.

2
Alarm · look now

Two independent sensors landing on the same wallet, case or person. It moved and no authorisation exists. That is not a coincidence, it is a contradiction.

3
Incident · open a file

A third source agrees. Movement, no authorisation, and a privileged read of the key material outside any authorised window. Now you have a person, and you never watched one.

What it would have cost to know

The alarm never needs to identify anybody. It only needs to notice that nobody was supposed to move that money.

01 / THE DESIGN CHOICE

Monitor the evidence, not the people

No employee is watched. The system watches assets under custody and the authorisations that should accompany them. A person only enters the picture when an asset moves and no authorisation explains it. That is the standard an evidence locker has had for a century.

02 / WHERE THE MODELS SIT

Beside the decision, never inside it

The rule is arithmetic and runs in microseconds. Four models from four vendors read the queue out of band, rank what deserves a human and write the narrative. A rate limit cannot block an alarm, and an agreeable model cannot dismiss one.

03 / WHY A GRAPH

The second hop is the whole argument

"Did the wallet move without authorisation" is one table. "Did anyone who opened this key material do anything else worth a look" is four joins in SQL and one traversal in a graph. Only the second question finds a person without watching everybody.

Everyone was watching the suspect.
Nobody was watching the evidence.

Custody observability is not a new category and it is not surveillance. It is the control an evidence locker has had for a century, applied to an asset that can be moved from a kitchen at three in the morning without the door making a sound.
